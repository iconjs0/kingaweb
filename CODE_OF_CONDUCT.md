# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive and professional community. Participation should make people feel safe to learn, question assumptions and contribute regardless of background, identity, experience or location.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, authorization and responsible disclosure.
- Welcome different levels of experience and communicate without gatekeeping.
- Accept feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption and unauthorized security testing are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Concerns should be reported privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled with discretion and conflicts of interest will be avoided where possible.
